> [On the magic system](#s "Others in this thread have suggested that the Spirit Caller magic is completely overpowered compared to all others. I... question that. The power of a Spirit Caller is never in the Spirit Caller themselves, but rather in the Spirits. (They seem inclined to treat a Caller well, but that's probably only because the Caller is the only one who can ever make what they want known to others... their only translator, so to speak). And the Spirits are not forced to obey the Caller, as evidenced by the cloth teashop spirit. Moreover, the Spirits clearly want *something* from the material world, as evidenced by the fact that they gather near the Caller in such profusion - some pretending to be just 'passing by'.")
> 
> [Further thoughts on magic](#s "So, when I think of what would have happened if Rowan had had the Spirit Caller magic instead of Sofia - Rowan would have mistreated the Spirits, used and abused them for his own ends. But the Spirits are explicitly *not* human. They trade in intangibles (with Dark Wizards and Oathkeepers), they can see intents, and they can escape to an alternate layer of reality. Moreover, the presence of prophecy - and the fact that a Forsworn Sister, who explicitly gets her abilities from Spirits, could see the future - implies that the Spirits do not exactly have the normal linear relationship with time.")
> 
> [On the nature of prophecy](#s "Prophecy is an interesting twist. It always begs the question - does it predict something that *will* happen, one way or the other, an inviolate fate that can never be altered, or does it imply the presence of some force that will *force* the prophesied Event to happen, one way or the other? Either way, it appears they can turn up unexpectedly - which raises the possibility that at least some of the Spirits are deliberately *steering* civilisation, for one or another reason. (They may even be responsible - via indirect means - for the current poor state of Dark Magic - perhaps they were getting mobbed and decided to reduce their customers to more manageable levels?) But it's interesting that they mediate *all* magic. I wonder what their ultimate aim is - are they perhaps no more than extradimensional tourists, are they trying to artificially increase the number of Spirit Callers, or are there several groups working at cross-purposes?")
