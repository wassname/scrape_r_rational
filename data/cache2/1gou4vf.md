>>>>>> Link first: [The Conversion Factor](https://imgur.com/gallery/conversion-factor-4-3-nb1qqf5)
>>>>>> > And what's cyoa?
>>>>>> In brief:
>>>>>> CYOAs-- "Choose Your Own Adventures," let you choose between multiple options to allow you to imagine some form of adventure. The CYOA I posted is a variant on the monster transformation genre, allowing you to customize a monstrous form for yourself in the context and constraints of a setting and plot. It does *not* include an actual story-- that's for you to imagine, or write, or roleplay about yourself. I did write a companion work and optional supplement-- ["Life After Conversion Day"](https://gaberockking.neocities.org/tcf_universe/lifeafterconversionday/lacd_interactive) that expands on the setting and options with some short stories. My full network of CYOAs is available on [my website](https://gaberockking.neocities.org/)
>>>>>> Now for the long explanation:
>>>>>> Have you ever been asked, "would you rather be able to fly or turn invisible?"
>>>>>> That's fundamentally what a CYOA is. You're presented with a number of (hopefully) interesting hypothetical choices, possibly in the context of a setting or story, intended to fuel your imagination by being interesting dilemmas. CYOA's appeal to the sort of people who like to read RPG sourcebooks and create D&D characters even if they never actually put them on the table.
>>>>>> It's pretty common for people to take their CYOA builds and then write a story about them, but by no means required. [Here's](https://slatestarcodex.com/2015/06/02/and-i-show-you-how-deep-the-rabbit-hole-goes/) a pretty archetypical example.
>>>>>> Modern CYOAs share a name with the original [Choose Your Own Adventure](https://www.cyoa.com/) books, but aren't a close relation. [Visual Novels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual_novel) bear the most similarity to the original format.
