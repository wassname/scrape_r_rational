>> [](#s "The plot is that they're exploring a tunnel system near their planet, and they can't get back until the planet completes their orbit. The tunnel system goes near a wormhole, and it turns out that it's some kind of interplanetary travel system. Some of the rationalist sections include the Capitalist getting annoyed that the electromagnet in his hat is pulling him towards the batteries he's trying to collect instead of the other way around, and he suggests that it's in backwards. The Student has him test it by turning around, and then asks him what he learned when he just ends up flying at the battery backwards. Sadly he doesn't get it and says that he learned the Student is not to be trusted. There was also a scene where the Cartographer explained that she's afraid of giving people unfinished maps, and once someone asked her about hurricanes. She told him what she knew, he thought that was everything, and it turned out that she underestimated it and he got hurt. She explains that knowledge about a hurricane is a map, even if it's not drawn on a piece of paper.")
