> Meta commentary: I am always a bit confused the writing community doesn't have good standards or ideas on how to handle rewriting content. Plenty of authors get in binds where sequels have to deal with mistakes and continuity errors - mistakes accumulate like radioactive waste in longer sequels. This does not need to be so! Mistakes could easily be dealt with if the original works were revised and re-released. Yet this is rarely, if ever done, and when the rare author _does_ issue a re-release, it's a low-status signal of a _bad_ author. Shouldn't the opposite be true - re-releasing is a sign of high conscientiousness and quality? Shouldn't there be acceptable, standard ways to rewrite a story? (I'm not talking about Editions here, those are _de facto_ methods of updating grammar/spelling/syntax and not semantic content).
