> Naruto: 
> 
> * [*Time Braid*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/5193644/1/Time-Braid) — one of the best [resetting time travel](http://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/GroundhogPeggySue) stories I’ve found so far (inb4: underage sex criticism);
> * [*Black Cloaks, Red Clouds*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/8654967/1/Black-Cloaks-Red-Clouds) — well written but in a gritty setting;
> * [*The Waves Arisen*](https://wertifloke.wordpress.com/2015/01/25/chapter-1/) — a well-written rat. \ munchkining story;
> * [*Marked for Death*](https://forums.sufficientvelocity.com/threads/marked-for-death-a-rational-naruto-quest.24481/) — I’ve only read the starting chapters so far but it looked like a promising story back then;
> * [*Shinobi, The RPG*](https://forums.spacebattles.com/threads/shinobi-the-rpg-naruto-si.380860/) — IIRC, starts good but eventually turns into a crackfic;
> * [*Chiaroscuro*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/11267384/1/Chiaroscuro), [*In the blood*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/6207715/1/In-The-Blood) — these have been positively mentioned here before (haven’t read them myself yet).
> 
> Harry Potter:
> 
> * [*Natural 20*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/8096183/1/Harry-Potter-and-the-Natural-20) — well-written HP × D&D, the author has [shown their work](http://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/ShownTheirWork) 
> * [*Well Groomed Mind*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/8163784/1/The-Well-Groomed-Mind) — HP joins VD *without* the story deteriorating into slashy teenage romance drama;
> * [*Too Young to Die*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/9057950/1/Too-Young-to-Die) — interesting and well-written story, even though the protagonist is not perfectly rational (he makes do with what he has, even when it’ll be harmful to his long-term mental well-being);
> * [*On the Way to Greatness*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/4745329/1/On-the-Way-to-Greatness) ;
> * [*Forging the Sword*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/3557725/1/Forging-the-Sword);
> * [*For Love of Magic*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/11669575/1/For-Love-of-Magic) — Harry’s into experimentation, research of magic, etc. Has smut and wishful thinking though;
> * [*Applied Cultural Anthropology*](https://www.fanfiction.net/s/9238861/1/Applied-Cultural-Anthropology-or) — the first 9 chapters that I’ve read were good.
