>> >The __Citigroup Center__ (formerly __Citicorp Center__ and now known as __601 Lexington Avenue__) is an office tower in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), located at [53rd Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/53rd_Street_(Manhattan\)) between [Lexington Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexington_Avenue) and [Third Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_Avenue_(Manhattan\)) in midtown [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan). It was built in 1977 to house the headquarters of [Citibank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citibank). It is 915 feet (279 m) tall, one of the ten tallest [skyscrapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyscraper) in New York, and has 59 floors with 1.3 million square feet (120,000 m²) of office space,
>> >The building is one of the most distinctive and imposing in New York's skyline, thanks to a 45° angled top and a unique [stilt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stilts)-style base. It was designed by architect [Hugh Stubbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Stubbins) and structural engineer [William LeMessurier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_LeMessurier). The building is currently owned by [Boston Properties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Properties), and in 2009, was renamed 601 Lexington Avenue.
