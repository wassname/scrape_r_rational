> I've been thinking about Fallout: Equestria. Specifically, the scene where [](#s "Fluttershy demonstrates her first healing megaspell. Not only do I find the idea that those soldiers would rise up to continue fighting to the death implausible, given their recent near-death experience, Fluttershy somehow got this secret project to the battlefield without anyone knowing what her ministry had been doing, or what she was going to do. Or that Fluttershy herself wouldn't consider that a battlefield is not the best place to test this new technology out, or that the megaspell technology wouldn't be used to amplify anything that Fluttershy would maybe be *not so okay* with. And then I wondered, if Fluttershy really wanted peace, why would she not plot for peace throughout the entire war, and do all she could to *ensure* that the world would not be destroyed?")
