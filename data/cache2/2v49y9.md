>>>>>> Regardless, you need to cast both the Contingency and the other spell at the same time.  A FLEA-graduate is probably a Wiz20/Sorc20/Archivist20.  Wiz and Sorc can cast the 6th level [Contingency](http://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/contingency.htm), archivists cannot.  Between your Wiz and Sorc classes you have 40 slots from 6-9th level, all of which can load Contingency.  If you wanted to burn your entire high-level spell list for a day you could have an N=40 chain of Contingencies; they would last for 20 days(1) and then you'd have to do it all again.  You can't use scrolls to increase N because you ['activate a scroll'](http://www.d20srd.org/srd/magicItems/scrolls.htm), you don't 'cast a spell from a scroll' and Contingency requires that both spells be *cast* at the same time.   You also can't use [Rings of Spell Storing](http://www.dandwiki.com/wiki/SRD:Ring_of_Spell_Storing) because those can't cast 6th level spells.  So, at least under 2YE rules, I think you're limited to N=40.
