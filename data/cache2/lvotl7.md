>>>>> **u/lillarty** [+8]  (4 days later)
>>>>> 
>>>>> I don't know anything about xianxia as I've still never read any, but xianxia seems to be a type of progression fantasy, which sort of by definition requires the protagonist to be powerless for a significant period of time, so that the power they have later contrasts strongly and the audience actually *feels* the growth; there would be very little impact if the protagonist went from powerful to slightly-more-powerful. 
>>>>> 
>>>>> Whether or not it's engaging in this powerless stage is largely dependent on writer skill, however, and as always Sturgeon's Law is in full effect.

>>>>> **u/Redditor76394** [+1]  (6 days later)
>>>>> 
>>>>> MC's have to be weak at the start, so they have very few tools to work with. They obviously can't have martial strength, and can't be rich either since gaining wealth is part of the progression fantasy. So your MCs aren't going to have any treasures to rely on either, meaning they only have their wits and social smarts.
>>>>> 
>>>>> Interesting plots where your MC overcomes obstacles using purely his own wits and cleverness are more difficult for an author to write, so most authors have boring beginnings because they can't give their MC much agency early on.

>>>> SCP is a collaborative writing project written by users all over the world, most of it in the form of classified files detailing ‘special containment procedures’ for various anomalous items. These files are from the SCP Foundation, an organisation dedicated to protecting the world from anomalous things.
>>>> 
>>>> The famous ones, off the top of my head, include SCP 682, a murderous unkillable lizard, or a statue that tries to break your neck if you take your eyes off it (073), or a box of pills that cures *anything*, or a staircase that goes down forever, or a booth which can alter the physical constants of the universe... things like that. It’s not all horror, although horror is a big part of it.
>>>> 
>>>> There’s a scp subreddit around, and the official page is [here](http://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/about-the-scp-foundation)
>>>> 
>>>> Probably the most relevant one to understand this series of short stories is SCP 055, which is a short and sweet classic-era SCP (the newer ones are a lot longer and have a lot more going on, for better or worse). Qntm must have liked the concept and tried to go further with it, resulting in the antimemetics stories I linked.
